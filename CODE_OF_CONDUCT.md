# Code of Conduct

AgentSpace follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Critique ideas and code, not people.
- **Welcome newcomers.** Every expert was once asking a "basic" question.
- **No harassment.** No insults, trolling, sexualised language or imagery, personal or political attacks, or publishing someone's private information. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any project space.

**Reporting.** If someone makes you feel unwelcome or unsafe, email the maintainers at **CONDUCT_CONTACT** (read only by the maintainers). Reports are handled privately and as fast as we can. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or ban people who break these rules, as described in the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
